# Studio artwork

`apex-mark.svg`, `paddock-car.svg`, `grid-livery.svg`, and `icons.svg` were drawn specifically for Apex LineUp. They contain original vector artwork, not official F1 or team logos. All files are local and work offline.

- Apex monogram: racing lines and a chequered corner.
- Paddock car: overhead open-wheel racing illustration with circuit linework.
- Grid livery: speed stripes and a finishing flag.
- Symbols: consistent control icons, podium wreath and detailed ranking car sprite.

These additions share the project's existing red, black, white, and metric colors. Decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology and does not intercept canvas interactions.
